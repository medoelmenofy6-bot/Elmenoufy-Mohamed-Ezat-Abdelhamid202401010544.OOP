2.1. Why are the variables declared as private?
To enforce Encapsulation and data security. Declaring variables as private prevents external classes from modifying or accessing the fields directly without validation.

2.2. What happens if the variables are declared public?
Any external class can freely modify or access the variables directly (e.g., student.cgpa = -5.0), leading to potential data corruption and security vulnerabilities.

2.3. Why do we use getters and setters?
Getters provide controlled read-only access to private variables, while setters provide controlled write access, allowing us to validate or restrict data before updating it.
